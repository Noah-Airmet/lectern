Check a quotation before you use it. Pass the exact words you intend to put in quotation marks, and the cite (id or url) you intend to attach. Join omitted stretches with "..." as you would in print.

Verdicts: exact and normalized pass (normalized forgives case, punctuation, hyphenation, ligatures and line breaks, never wording). elsewhere means the words are in that book but not on the cited page; the result gives the right page. near_miss means something close is there but not these words; the result shows the page's own words and the difference, so quote those instead. not_found means the words are not there. A famous line is often quoted in a later paraphrase: check it against the page, and quote the page.

Without cite, pass doc to check against a whole book, or neither to search the whole library for the passage. The check is code, not a model: deterministic and exact. The text is OCR, so a near_miss on an old spelling or a misread letter is worth a look at the page image.
